Honest Cryptographic Limits & Real-World Trade-Offs
True security demands complete honesty. End-to-end encryption is extremely powerful, but it is not magic. Here are the genuine limits you should understand:
• Lost passphrase and recovery code means lost data: Because we have zero backdoor and zero access to your master keys, if you lose both your passphrase and recovery code, your encrypted data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
• E2EE doesn't protect an already-infected computer while the key is unlocked: If malware, a keylogger, or memory dumper compromises your local device while your master key is in RAM, it could intercept plaintext data before encryption occurs.
• No server-side scanning, search or previews: Since our cloud storage only holds ciphertext, threat detection, anomaly scans, and integrity monitoring must run locally on your endpoint.
• Backup size and timing metadata may still be visible: Network observers and cloud providers can still observe file sizes, backup frequency, and transmission timestamps even when content and names are fully encrypted.
• Immutable storage and two-key approval are still needed to stop deletion: Encryption prevents unauthorized reading, but separate immutable storage policies and Two-Key phone approvals are necessary to stop malicious ransomware wipers from deleting backups.
• Demonstration vs. Production architecture: This suite runs directly in your browser using the official Web Crypto API (crypto.subtle). A production business deployment needs a persistent background service agent, a reviewed Argon2id implementation, and an independent third-party security audit.
